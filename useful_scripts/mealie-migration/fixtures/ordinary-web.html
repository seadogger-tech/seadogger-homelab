Pantry Tomato Rice

Synthetic recipe fixture. Yield: 4 servings.

Preparation: 5 minutes. Cooking: 20 minutes. Total: 25 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup long-grain rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice.
  2. Combine the rice, water, salt and tomato paste in a saucepan.
  3. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes.
